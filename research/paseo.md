## Summary
- Screen: FAIL at N2
- Paseo is active and popular. The repo is not archived and has no deprecation notice. It had 1,162 non-bot commits to `main` in the last 90 days, v0.11.4 shipped on 2026-10-15 and v0.12.0-beta.3 on 2026-10-18, and it has 21.3k stars. N3 and N1 pass for getpaseo/paseo.
- By its own account it is a one-person project. The README says it is "built by one person and funded by the people who use it". paseo.sh/sponsor says "It is maintained by one person, full time". CONTRIBUTING says product decisions "remain with the maintainer", that "Pull requests are closed by default", and that "I may reopen the ones I want to take forward".
- No primary source shows a second human merging PRs or publishing releases in the window (2026-04-22 → 2026-10-19):
  - Mohamed Boudra (`boudra`) wrote 2,444 of the 2,844 non-bot commits (85.9%). The next human wrote 1.2%.
  - He is the only human committer on `main`.
  - All 11 merged PRs I sampled (April–October) were merged by `boudra` or the `paseo-bot` GitHub App.
  - He tagged every release. GitHub Releases are published by github-actions. Every npm version in the window was published by `boudra`, the only npm maintainer. The App Store seller is "Mohamed Boudra Ziani".
- The separate relay repo, getpaseo/paseo-relay (it runs relay.paseo.sh), also fails N1 (109 stars) and N2 (all 32 commits are by Mohamed Boudra). The relay is optional: "Relay is off on new installations", and direct TCP or Tailscale connections work. So the relay alone would not sink Paseo. The N2 fail of the core repo does.
- I did not run C1, A1 or A3/A4: the screen stops at the first hard fail (7.11). The Stage 1 leads (≈21.3k stars, Apache-2.0, "built by one person", GitHub Sponsors) are all confirmed.
