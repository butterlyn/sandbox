## Open questions
- **Does N2 hold in spirit?** The project calls itself "single-maintainer" in three places. Every second-human merge falls on 2026-10-14 or later and was made by one core reviewer. Releases, tags, secrets and publisher identities stay with chernistry, and the charter says "Releases and protected paths wait" when he is away.
  - Under the strict alternative (self-description decisive), N2 → FAIL. That gives `SCREEN FAIL at N2`, and Bernstein could not be used even as glue (7.12).
  - My sample covered 17 PRs. A full census of mergers (blocked: api.github.com) could find more second-human merges, which would only strengthen the PASS.
- **A1 (iii) at stack level.** Could a screened iOS terminal (7.19 `ios-terminal`) plus tmux in WSL2 cover (iii)? The user would `cd` to a repo and run `bernstein … --cli claude|codex`, or use `bernstein run-service submit`.
  - 7.4 counts a terminal only when it is "attached to the native TUI"; here the harnesses run headless. The orchestrator must decide whether Bernstein's CLI/TUI (`bernstein live`) qualifies. If it does, A1 can be cleared at stack level; Bernstein alone stays PARTIAL.
  - The GitHub App path (`src/bernstein/github_app`: issues to tasks, which would be reachable from GitHub Mobile) was not examined. If it lets the user choose the harness and the repo, it might cover (iii).
- **A1 (ii): does steer guidance ever reach a running agent mid-run?** The source shows delivery through prompt rendering at spawn. A mid-run path, such as a hook or an MCP read tool, would raise (ii) toward PASS.
- **Deep-dive: is the Claude transcript kept? (A2, A6b, F5)** The spawn always passes `--no-session-persistence` (claude.py L492). Yet the adapter declares `supports_session_continuation` through `--resume` and says Claude "writes a JSONL transcript per session under `~/.claude/projects/…`" (claude.py L287–296).
  - The live log drops tool results.
  - Unless Bernstein keeps the raw stream-json somewhere, A2 (tool call results in the UI), A6b (native transcript) and F5 (`claude --resume`) are at risk for Claude sessions.
  - Codex keeps its own `$CODEX_HOME/sessions`. That is not verified here.
- **Networking.** `--tunnel-provider auto` may pick Cloudflare quick tunnels, ngrok or bore, which are unscreened third-party networking. The stack should pin `--tunnel-provider tailscale` (Tailscale is screened as `tailwind`) or reach the dashboard over the tailnet.
